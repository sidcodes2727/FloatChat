The Argo program is a global array of nearly 4,000 robotic profiling floats measuring ocean temperature, salinity, and biogeochemical parameters. Each float operates on a autonomous 10-day cycle: it descends to a drift depth of about 1,000 meters to drift with ocean currents, then drops further to 2,000 meters before ascending to the surface. During ascent, sensors record vertical profiles. At the surface, data is transmitted to shore via satellite (such as Iridium or Argos) and GPS coordinates are logged.
